  • The Pomodoro Technique

    • The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method that splits your study time into short, focused sessions. You study for 25 minutes, then take a 5 minute break. After 4 rounds, you take a longer break.
    Studying for hours without rest leads to tiredness and loss of focus. Short sessions make big tasks easier to start, and regular breaks keep your mind fresh.

  • Test yourself

    • Testing yourself with flashcards helps you remember more than reading notes again. Instead of just recognizing information, you practice recalling it from memory, which is exactly what you need during an exam. Write a question on one side of a card and the answer on the other. Go through the cards often, and keep the ones you get wrong in a separate pile to review more. Combine this with Pomodoro rounds: study for 25 minutes, then use the first minutes of the next round to quiz yourself.
